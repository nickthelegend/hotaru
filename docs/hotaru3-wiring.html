Everything that lives in the tub, and what connects to what, exactly as the HOTARU 3 firmware expects it (firmware/hotaru/config.h). Hold the button and talk: the lamp answers out loud and acts the answer out.

On the PCA9685 headers, brown goes on the GND row at the board edge, red on V+, orange on PWM. Leave slack at every joint: the turntable pans ±90° and the arm folds, so a tight lead will pull. Calibration lives on the lamp: cal shows it, cal save keeps changes.

Power and first switch-on

Power rules

  • The servos get their own 5 V supply, 3 A or more, on the PCA9685's V+ terminal and nothing else.
  • All grounds join: servo supply, ESP32, PCA9685, amp, mic, ring, button.
  • 2200 µF across V+ and GND at the PCA9685 terminal, stripe to GND.
  • The servo supply's + must not touch the ESP32's 5V or 3V3 or the PCA9685's VCC.
  • On a laptop's USB alone, hold the MX key while plugging in: the servos stay off. Starting four MG996Rs from USB makes the laptop cut the port.

First power-up

  1. Put your Wi-Fi and keys in secrets.h, then flash firmware/hotaru (ESP32S3 Dev Module, OPI PSRAM, Huge APP).
  2. Park the arm folded at rest and power up: the servos engage one at a time, it stretches awake and says hello.
  3. Hold the MX key, speak after the beep, let go. A short tap plays a random gesture.
  4. Serial monitor at 115200: list shows all 60 actions, ask <text> talks in text, cal shows the calibration. If it prints no PCA9685 at 0x40, check SDA, SCL and VCC.
